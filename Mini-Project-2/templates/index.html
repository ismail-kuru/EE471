Click “Speak”, say a command, then click again to send it.

What can I say?
  • Create: “Create item, type book, title The Catcher in the Rye, author J.D. Salinger, pages 220” or “Add a DVD called Alien, directed by Ridley Scott, 117 minutes”
  • List: “List all items”, “Show available DVDs” or “Which books are checked out?”
  • Get one: “Get item 3” or “Tell me about item 3”
  • Replace: “Replace item 3, title Emma, author Jane Austen, pages 400”
  • Change: “Update item 3, pages 200” or “Change the title of item 3 to Emma”
  • Delete: “Delete item 3” or “Remove item 3”
  • Check out: “Check out item 3” or “Borrow item 3”
  • Return: “Return item 3” or “Bring back item 3”
